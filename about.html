Meet Meghan Brooks

Direct, in-home access to an experienced Board Certified Behavior Analyst — no waitlists, no intermediaries, just focused support for your family.

Meghan Brooks

M.S., BCBA, NCLBA

Strategies you understand. Confidence you carry forward.

Meghan Brooks founded Brooks Behavioral Group to give families direct access to an experienced BCBA in the place where daily challenges arise: their own home. She works alongside parents and caregivers through the routines that matter most, from morning transitions and toileting to meals and bedtime.

Each engagement begins with an understanding of the household's routines, priorities, and needs. Meghan develops practical strategies tailored to the individual and provides hands-on guidance to parents, caregivers, and household staff so they can use those strategies with confidence between visits.

Through her private-pay practice, Meghan serves families in Kernersville and the greater Piedmont Triad. Families work directly with her and receive personalized support where they need it most.

Credentials
Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA), M.S. North Carolina Licensed Behavior Analyst (NCLBA)
Who We Serve
Individuals ages 2 and up — no upper age cutoff Kernersville, NC and the greater Piedmont Triad
