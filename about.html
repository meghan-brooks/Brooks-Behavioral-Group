Meet Meghan Brooks

Meghan Brooks

M.S., BCBA, NCLBA

Clinical Expertise. Practical Leadership. Meaningful Progress.

Brooks Behavioral Group was founded by Meghan Brooks, M.S., BCBA, NCLBA, a Board Certified Behavior Analyst with experience spanning direct clinical services, behavioral assessment, caregiver coaching, clinical leadership, and organizational operations.

Throughout her career, Meghan has held roles including Clinical Director, Center Director, and Director of Operations, providing firsthand experience in the clinical and operational challenges faced by behavioral healthcare organizations.

Her areas of professional focus include behavioral consultation, staff training and development, caregiver engagement, clinical quality improvement, and the implementation of practical systems that support consistent service delivery.

Through Brooks Behavioral Group, Meghan combines her clinical background and leadership experience to provide individualized support to families and strategic consultation to organizations.

Her approach emphasizes practical solutions, sustainable systems, and the confidence to implement meaningful change.
