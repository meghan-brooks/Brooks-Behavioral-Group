01 / Discover

The Home Consultation

$350 One-time consultation, approximately 90 minutes

A personalized starting point for families seeking clarity around a specific behavioral challenge or everyday routine.

Includes

  • Caregiver interview and review of concerns
  • In-home observation of relevant routines
  • Identification of environmental and behavioral factors
  • Initial recommendations and practical next steps
  • Brief written consultation summary
Book a Home Consultation

02 / Refine

The Routine Reset

Starting at $1,250 Approximately 3–4 weeks

Focused support for one challenging routine, such as bedtime, toileting, morning routines, or transitions.

Includes

  • Initial in-home consultation
  • Personalized written strategy plan
  • Two in-home implementation and coaching sessions
  • Caregiver modeling and guided practice
  • One virtual follow-up
  • Strategy adjustments based on progress and family feedback
Explore the Routine Reset

03 / Transform

The Family Framework

Starting at $2,500 Approximately 6–8 weeks

Comprehensive support for families seeking greater consistency across multiple routines and caregivers.

Includes

  • Comprehensive home and caregiver consultation
  • Individualized strategies across 2–3 priority routines
  • Four in-home implementation and coaching sessions
  • Parent and household caregiver coaching
  • Written implementation resources
  • Two virtual follow-ups
  • Final review and maintenance recommendations

A Different Approach to Support

Private care. Personalized possibilities.

BBG operates exclusively as a private-pay consulting practice. No insurance authorization is required, and families are not expected to commit to long-term treatment plans.

Each engagement is designed around specific priorities, with an emphasis on practical strategies, caregiver confidence, and meaningful progress in everyday life.

  • Private pay only
  • Direct access to a BCBA
  • No long-term treatment commitment
