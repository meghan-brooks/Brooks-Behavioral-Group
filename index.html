The BBG Approach

A thoughtful process. Built around real life.

Meaningful change doesn't come from handing families a list of recommendations. It comes from truly understanding what's happening in your home, building practical, individualized strategies, and helping the people who know your child best feel confident using them — so progress lasts.

01

Understand

Before changing the routine, Meghan learns the routine.

It begins with listening. Meghan talks with you about your concerns, priorities, what you've already tried, and what everyday life currently looks like for your family. When appropriate, she observes the routine in the environment where it actually happens.

The Difference

Recommendations are grounded in your family's actual experience, not simply what should work in theory.

02

Personalize

A strategy only works if it works for your family.

Once the challenge is understood, Meghan translates that information into practical, individualized strategies designed around your child, your home, and the people implementing them. You'll know what to do, when to do it, and why you're doing it — without an overwhelming plan filled with clinical language.

The Difference

Evidence-based strategies that feel realistic enough to use on an ordinary Tuesday — not just during a consultation.

03

Practice

Knowing the strategy is different from feeling confident using it.

Meghan models strategies, coaches you as you practice them, answers questions in real time, and helps troubleshoot the moments that don't go according to plan. Parents, grandparents, nannies, and other consistent caregivers can be included so your child receives clear, predictable support from the people in everyday life.

The Difference

You're not simply given recommendations and expected to figure out implementation on your own — you practice together.

04

Sustain

Success means you won't need BBG forever.

As strategies begin working, Meghan looks at what's improving, what's still difficult, and what needs to be adjusted — helping you refine the approach, plan for new situations, and recognize when a strategy should evolve as your child grows. Most importantly, you'll understand the principles behind each strategy so you can apply them beyond one routine or one challenge.

The Difference

BBG is designed to build caregiver capability — not dependence on ongoing professional support.

The Expertise Stays With You

Real support. Lasting progress.

BBG combines behavioral expertise with something equally important: implementation in real life. You receive direct access to an experienced BCBA, hands-on coaching, and a clear path toward greater independence.
